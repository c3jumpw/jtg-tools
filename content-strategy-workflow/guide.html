Who does what

The three roles

Every person has one role, set by an admin when they're invited. Your role decides which accounts you see and what you can change.

Admin

Runs the workspace.

  • Sees every active account
  • Invites people and sets their access
  • Turns accounts on and off
  • Moves entries between accounts
  • Sees private Notebook links

Team member

Produces content. Shown as rep in the invite form.

  • Sees only their assigned accounts
  • Creates, edits and moves entries
  • Edits Brand Foundation and prompts
  • Connects their own ClickUp login

Client

Follows and contributes to their own content.

  • Sees only their business(es)
  • Adds ideas and new entries
  • Uploads their own assets
  • Comments and messages the team
  • No ClickUp account needed

First time

Getting in

Everyone signs in with the email and password tied to their invitation. Admins and team members then link their ClickUp login once on each device.

Main menu

Finding your way around

The dark bar across the top is the same on every screen. Tabs on the left switch views; controls on the right act on the whole app.

Client quick start

What clients do most

You see your own business's content, live. You can't break anything: stages, dates and assignments are your team's to change.

See where everything stands

  1. Open Board.
  2. Each column is a stage; each card is a piece of content.
  3. Tap a card to see its files and comments.

Send the team a new request

  1. Tap + New at the top.
  2. Type what you want made and tap Create.
  3. It lands in the first stage (Idea) for your team to pick up.

Share photos, logos or reference

  1. Open the content card.
  2. Under Client Assets, tap + Upload.
  3. The upload is noted in the Log so the team sees it.

Give feedback or approve

  1. Open the card.
  2. Write in Log an update… and tap Post.
  3. For questions not tied to one post, use Messages on the Dashboard.

Jot down a raw idea

  1. Go to Ideas and tap + Capture.
  2. Pick Idea, Link or Note and save.
  3. When it's ready to be made, tap Promote.

Switch between your businesses

  1. Use the account switcher at the top (only shown with 2+ businesses).
  2. All my businesses mixes everything together.
  3. Pick one name to focus on it.
Tip+ New creates real work that appears on the Board straight away. + Capture in Ideas saves a raw thought that only reaches the Board when someone promotes it.

Overview

Dashboard

The Dashboard answers “what needs me today?” for whatever is selected in the account filter. Click any row to open that entry.

TipIdea pipeline and Messages only appear when one account is selected, because ideas and messages belong to a single account.

The stages

How an entry moves to Published

Every content entry sits in one stage. Stage names and colours come straight from the ClickUp list, so if they change in ClickUp, ContentFlow follows. These are the current stages and how teams typically use them.

Idea

New requests and promoted ideas land here first.

Copy – In Progress

Caption, script or article being written.

Media – In Progress

Photos, video or graphics being made.

For Review

Ready to check. ContentFlow looks for a draft or raw media first.

Approved

Signed off and ready to schedule.

Scheduled

Queued for its Scheduled Date.

Published

Live. Never counted as stuck.

For Review checkMoving an entry into For Review when it has no files under Copy / Drafts or Media Files (raw) asks “Move to For Review anyway?” It's a reminder, not a block.
ClientsYou'll see each entry's stage on the Board and inside the entry. Your team moves entries between stages; when something reaches For Review, that's usually your cue to look and comment.

Where new entries come from

+ New (top bar)→Entry in the first stage + Add task (Board)→Blank entry form Promote (Idea Inbox)→Entry with the idea's details

Workflow view

Board

One column per stage, one card per entry. It's the fastest way to see what's moving and to move things along.

ClientsYour Board is view-only: cards can't be dragged, and only stages that currently hold your content appear as columns. Tap any card to open it.

Workflow view

List

The same entries as a table. Best for finding one thing fast or scanning dates across many entries.

ClientsYour List shows Name, Status, Account and Mediums.

Workflow view

Calendar

A month view of everything with a date. Entries are coloured by stage, so a red-orange chip next week means something is still waiting on review.

TipAn entry with neither date set won't appear here. Open it and fill in Scheduled Date or Publish Date.

Workflow view

Feed

Every Log comment across the entries you're looking at, newest first. Catch up on a week of conversation without opening each entry.

The detail view

Inside a content entry

Click any card, row or feed line to open the entry. Everything about one piece of content is here. Field edits are saved together when you press Save changes; Log posts and file uploads save immediately.

What clients see

Tap any card or row to open the entry. You'll see where it stands, its shared files, and the conversation about it.

Attachments

Files and who sees them

Each entry has six file categories. Four are working space for the team; two are shared with the client. Files are stored privately in ContentFlow, and clicking a file name downloads it.

Team only

  • Copy / DraftsCaptions, scripts, article drafts.
  • NotesBriefs, research, call notes.
  • Media ExtractionsClips and stills pulled from longer media.
  • Media Files (raw)Original footage and photos. Promoted ideas drop their attachments here.

Shared with the client

  • Published / FinalThe finished piece. Uploaded by the team.
  • Client AssetsLogos, photos and reference from the client. Anyone can upload here.
Admin & teamUpload to any category and delete with the ✕ beside a file. Every upload posts a line to the Log, such as “Uploaded ‘reel-v2.mp4’ to Media Files (raw).”
ClientsYou can upload to Client Assets and download anything shown to you. To remove a file you added by mistake, ask your team in the Log.

Ideas › Foundation

Brand Foundation

The account's strategy on one page: positioning, audience, offers, voice, and ready-to-copy lines. Keep several profiles as the brand evolves; one is always marked current.

What a profile holds

PositioningNiche, what the business does, elevator pitch, value proposition
AudienceBuckets, each with demographics, psychographics, pain points and goals
Expertise & OfferExpertise areas; offers with features, advantages and benefits; proof points
Voice & KeywordsVoice words, what to avoid, keywords, CTA lines and hook lines (one per line)
Social LinksInstagram, Facebook, TikTok, X, YouTube and Pinterest take the username. LinkedIn takes the full profile URL.
NotebookAdmin-only link to private notes
Prompt LibraryReusable prompts that fill in from this profile (next section)
TipPaste a full profile link or an @handle into a social field and ContentFlow trims it to the username. Deleting the current profile makes the next most recent one current, so an account always has one.
ClientsThe Foundation is read-only for you, and every Copy button works. If something is out of date, tell your team in Messages.

Settings › Admin panel

Admin panel

Two tabs: Accounts controls which client accounts are live in ContentFlow, and Invite & Users controls who can sign in and what they see. Back to board returns you to the app.

Heads-upAccess is set when you send the invite, so include every account the person needs. The panel can't yet add an account to someone who's already been invited.

Step by step

Common admin jobs

Onboard a new client

  1. In ClickUp, open the client in Companies & Accounts and copy its MKC ID.
  2. ContentFlow: Settings › Admin panel › Invite & Users.
  3. Enter their email and name, role client, paste the MKC ID. Running several businesses? Add each ID, comma-separated.
  4. Send invite.
  5. On Accounts, check the account's switch is on.
  6. Fill in the Brand Foundation and Pillars & Rhythm so they arrive to a ready workspace.

Add a team member

  1. Invite & Users, role rep, with the MKC ID of each account they'll work on.
  2. After signing in they'll connect their own ClickUp login. It must already have access to the Client Master Content Strategy Workflow list in ClickUp.
To make another admin, choose role admin; no MKC ID needed.

Pause a client account

  1. Admin panel › Accounts, search for it.
  2. Switch it off. It disappears from every filter and its team members and clients lose access.
  3. Switch it back on to restore everything as it was.

Move an entry to the right account

  1. Open the entry.
  2. Click the account badge above the title and choose the account.
  3. Save changes.
An entry belongs to the account whose MKC ID matches its Related Account ID field in ClickUp.

Keep private reference notes

  1. For one entry: fill in its Notebook Link field in ClickUp. It shows in the entry for admins.
  2. For a whole brand: Ideas › Foundation › Edit › Notebook, then Save.
Team members and clients never see Notebook links.

Check what a role sees

  1. Use the role switch at the top of this guide.
  2. Every screen above redraws to show only that role's view and controls.

When something looks off

Troubleshooting

“Could not reach ClickUp” or the board looks out of date

Open Settings › Sync now. If the message comes back, choose Disconnect ClickUp, then connect again with the ClickUp login that has access to the workflow list.

Something I changed in ClickUp isn't showing

ContentFlow loads from ClickUp when you open it. Settings › Sync now pulls the latest without reloading.

The Ideas tab says “Pick an account”

Ideas belong to one account. Choose that account in the filter at the top.

I see “Almost there” after signing in

Your login works, but your admin hasn't linked your business yet. It fills in by itself once they do.

An account is missing from my filter

Either it's switched off in the Admin panel, or you aren't assigned to it. Ask an admin.

I can't drag cards on my phone

Touch screens don't support dragging here. Open the entry and change Status. For ideas, open the idea, tap Edit and choose a pillar.

It asked “Move to For Review anyway?”

The entry has no files under Copy / Drafts or Media Files (raw). Upload the draft first, or confirm to move it anyway.

There's no Upload button in some file sections

Clients upload to Client Assets. Published / Final is filled by your team.

The Notebook link isn't showing on an entry

It appears only when the entry's Notebook Link field is filled in ClickUp, and only for admins.

An entry isn't on the Calendar

The Calendar uses Publish Date, then Scheduled Date. Fill in one of them.

My new email address isn't working yet

Click the confirmation link sent to the new address. Until then, sign in with the old one.

Words used in ContentFlow

Glossary

Entry
One piece of content in production. Called a task in ClickUp and on some buttons.
Stage
Where an entry is in the workflow (Idea through Published). Shown as Status.
Medium
Where it will be posted: Instagram Reel, Blog, Email Blast and so on.
Account
A client business. Matched to entries by its MKC ID.
MKC ID
An account's ID from ClickUp's Companies & Accounts list, used when inviting people.
Next Action Date
When the next step on an entry is due. Drives Overdue, Due this week and Stuck.
Stuck
An open entry with no Next Action Date and no activity for 7+ days.
Pillar
One of the 3–5 themes an account posts about.
Rhythm
An account's idea source, weekly time and routine.
Idea
Raw material in the Idea Inbox. Not production work until promoted.
Promote
Turn an idea into a production entry.
Foundation profile
A saved version of the brand strategy. ★ marks the current one.
Prompt tag
A placeholder like {{keywords}} filled from the Foundation.
Log
An entry's comment thread, shared with ClickUp.
Messages
The account-wide conversation on the Dashboard.
Notebook
An admin-only link to private reference notes.
