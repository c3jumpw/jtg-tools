Quick start

  1. Bring something in. Paste a screenshot, drop files on the slide, or use Open ▾ for a PDF or a batch of photos.
  2. Mark it up. Pick Circle, Rectangle or Arrow and drag over the spot — even on top of a picture. The tool stays on until you press Esc.
  3. Explain it. Use the Text tool for labels on the slide, and the slide's notes for longer commentary.
  4. Export. Export ▾ → HTML, PNG or PDF, then choose all slides or just some.

Where things are

Open ▾
A PDF or photos as slides, or a saved HaloDeck project.
Save project
Downloads an editable copy — your backup, and how you move a deck to another device.
Export ▾
HTML, PNG or PDF, with a slide picker (All, Current or a list like 1-3, 5).
Right-click
On the slide (touch and hold on a phone): cut, copy, paste here, duplicate, arrange, lock, delete.
Right panel
Style for the selected item, Layers to reorder, hide or lock, Slide for page size, background and notes.
Slides panel
Add, duplicate, delete and drag slides into order.

Good to know

  • Your deck saves itself in this browser. Use Save project for a backup or to work on another device.
  • Hollow circles and boxes only catch clicks on their outline, so what's inside stays clickable.
  • Text hidden under a picture? Double-click it to edit.
  • Opening a PDF needs an internet connection the first time.
