Data Maintenance

One-time/occasional bulk fixes. These write directly to ClickUp — read what each one does before running it.

Backfill Related Account IDs
Scans every Master CRM Task. For any task missing its Related Account field that's linked to a Lead/Contact/Deal which itself has an Account, fills it in. Tasks that already have an Account set, or whose linked entry has none, are left untouched.
Check Team Directory ↔ ClickUp Sync
Compares every "active" row in the Team Directory sheet (by Workspace Email) against ClickUp's actual current workspace member list. Flags anyone active in the sheet with no matching real ClickUp account, and any ClickUp member not yet in the sheet at all. Read-only — doesn't change anything, just reports drift.

CRM Dictation Reader

Paste a cleaned recap from your CRM Dictation Cleaner prompt (or any structured notes). The reader extracts distinct operations — new contacts, logged activities, tasks, comments — and shows you a preview to confirm, adjust, or skip each one before anything is written to the CRM.
